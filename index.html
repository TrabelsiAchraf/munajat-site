Built for daily remembrance

Everything you need to keep the habit, nothing you don't.

📖

133 categories

Morning, evening and sleep adhkar plus Qur'an, travel, food, healing, Hajj and more — every situation covered.

✨

Authentic source

Every dhikr comes from Hisn al-Muslim by Sheikh Saʿīd al-Qaḥṭānī. References shown when available.

🔢

Tap-to-count

A built-in counter for every dhikr. Tap the big circle to increment, progress bar shows where you are.

🎧

Audio recitations

Professional recitations streamed from hisnmuslim.com. Tap Listen to hear the dhikr while you read it.

🌐

Trilingual

Arabic original with full vocalisation, plus French and English translation and transliteration. Pick your language in Settings.

❤️

Favorites

Tap the heart on any category card to keep it one tap away in your Favorites tab.

🔍

Search

Find a dhikr by title, category or Arabic text. Works fully offline.

🔔

Customizable reminders

Set the exact times for morning, evening and sleep adhkar reminders. Local notifications, no server involved.

🔥

Daily streak

A streak counter lights up the moment you read your first dhikr of the day. Skip a day, lose the streak.

📲

Home Screen widget

The suggested dhikr for the current period (morning, day, evening) — visible without opening the app.

📤

Share as a card

Generate a beautifully formatted image of any dhikr, ready to send to loved ones in any messaging app.

🔒

Privacy first

No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Everything is stored on your device. Audio loads from the web with no identifier.
